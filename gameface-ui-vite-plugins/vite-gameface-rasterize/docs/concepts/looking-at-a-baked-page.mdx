---
title: Looking at a baked page
---

Open your page in the Gameface Player, then open **`localhost:9444`** in Chrome. The Player runs
its debugger on that port by default and serves a full devtools frontend from it - no flags and
nothing to set up.

You get the real computed styles for the page as the engine sees it, which turns most of the
remaining silent failures into things you can look at directly.

## What to look for

**Did this element get a texture?** A baked element carries `data-rz-id`. If it is missing, the
runtime did not match it - the build's coverage line and RZ022 will have said so, and the reason
is usually its class list.

```js
document.querySelectorAll('[data-rasterize]').length     // marked
document.querySelectorAll('[data-rz-underlay]').length   // textured
```

**What was actually captured?** The texture is the `background-image` (or `border-image-source`) on
the `<i data-rz-underlay>` child. Open that URL on its own to see the picture the pipeline took. An
asset that looks blank, clipped or rotated tells you what went wrong at capture time.

**Which declarations came from the plugin?** They are all in `dist/rz/rasterize.css`, and they show
up in the computed-styles panel against their generated selector, so you can see exactly what was
stripped from your element and what replaced it.

## The build's own output

Three files are written next to the textures:

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `rz/rasterize.manifest.json` | every asset: mode, size, ink overflow, slice insets, live parts, hash |
| `rz/rasterize.diagnostics.json` | every diagnostic in full, with every element it applies to |
| `rz/report/<route>.png` | the page as shipped, the page without the bake, and their difference |

The diagnostics file is the one to reach for when the console summary groups something you need to
see element by element. Nothing in it is truncated.

## Reproducing a capture by hand

`--debug` writes every capture, before and after trimming, plus the geometry that produced it:

```shell
npx gameface-rasterize bake dist --debug
```

They land in `node_modules/.cache/rasterize/debug/`, one PNG per asset per state and a JSON beside
each with the plan, the measured ink overflow, and the placement the isolation computed. If an
asset looks wrong in the page, this says whether it was captured wrong or placed wrong.
